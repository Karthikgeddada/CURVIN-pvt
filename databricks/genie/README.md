# Genie spaces

Conversational access to CURVIN's curated data for non-technical
stakeholders. Each Genie space binds to specific gold tables and
carries curated instructions, definitions and sample questions.

Conventions:

- One space per business domain (e.g. Company Intelligence, Market
  Intelligence) rather than a single monolithic space.
- Spaces only read gold tables — never silver or bronze.
- Curated context (metric definitions, glossary, guardrails) is
  versioned here, not edited ad hoc in the UI.

No Genie spaces exist yet; document each (domain, tables, curators)
here as it is created.
