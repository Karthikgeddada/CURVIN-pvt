# Gold — curated business data

The product contract. Gold tables answer business questions: company
profiles, financial health, market positions, risk signals. They are
versioned, documented, and consumed by the CURVIN backend and Genie
spaces.

Rules:

- Gold tables expose stable, documented schemas — changing them is a
  coordinated product decision, never a silent migration.
- Metrics computed here follow definitions agreed with stakeholders;
  each metric's formula is documented in the table description.

No gold tables exist yet; document each table (purpose, grain,
consumers) here as it is created.
