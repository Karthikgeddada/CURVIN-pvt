# Product

CURVIN is enterprise-grade global company intelligence: a calm,
trustworthy surface for connected business, market, financial and
risk insight about companies worldwide.

## Product principles

1. **Signal over noise.** Every screen answers a real question;
   nothing exists to fill space.
2. **Calm, deliberate interactions.** Apple-inspired restraint —
   whitespace, hierarchy, purposeful motion.
3. **Trust through transparency.** Show where data came from and
   when; never fabricate confidence.
4. **Enterprise expectations.** Security, auditability and
   reliability are features, not add-ons.

## Milestone plan

| Milestone | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| M0 — Foundation (this repo) | Monorepo, frontend and backend foundations, data platform skeleton, CI-ready tooling |
| M1 — Company Search | Search and resolve companies against registry data; company profile pages |
| M2 — Company Intelligence | Rich profiles: structure, filings, financials, key people |
| M3 — Market Intelligence | Industry views, peers, market positions |
| M4 — Risk Intelligence | Risk signals, monitoring, alerts |
| M5 — Conversational access | Genie spaces over gold data |

Milestones are delivered independently — each one ships value on its
own. Details are elaborated per milestone; this document tracks scope
boundaries, not implementations.
